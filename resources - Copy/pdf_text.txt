1. AI-Based Material Test Certificate (MTC) Verification system 
Background 
Manufacturing and engineering organizations receive Mill Test Certificates (MTCs)  from 
multiple suppliers and mills, often in different formats, layouts, and levels of clarity. Manual 
verification of these documents against applicable standards is time -consuming, error-prone, 
and heavily dependent on individual expertise. 
Challenge 
Design and develop an AI-powered system capable of automatically reading, interpreting, and 
validating Material Test Certificates (MTCs) against defined material standards or customer-
specific requirements. 
The solution should be able to: 
• Extract key data such as: 
✓ Chemical composition 
✓ Mechanical properties 
✓ Heat / batch numbers 
✓ Test values and units 
• Interpret data from multiple document formats  (PDFs, scanned images, different 
layouts) 
• Compare extracted values against: 
✓ International standards (e.g., ASME, ASTM, MIL) or 
✓ User-defined material specifications 
Expected Outcome: 
The proposed solution should: 
• Flag compliance and non-compliance clearly 
• Reduce manual verification effort and review time 
• Minimize typographical and interpretation errors 
• Create a digital traceability and audit trail for material validation

16. Hydraulic press running cost reduction 
 
Problem Statement 
The hydraulic press currently consumes high energy during operation, particularly in the return 
stroke where significant power is required to lift the ram weight against gravity. During this 
upward movement, a considerable amount of energy is wasted, leading to increased operating 
and running costs. The existing system does not effectively recover or optimize the energy 
used during the return stroke. A solution  is required to minimize energy loss during the return 
cycle through design modification, system optimization, or adoption of an improved hydraulic 
concept. 
 
 
Deliverables 
The project shall propose feasible technical solutions to reduce energy consumption during the 
return stroke of the hydraulic press. This may include design modifications, hydraulic circuit 
optimization, energy recovery systems, counterbalance arrangements, or new press concepts. 
A comparative analysis of current versus proposed energy consumption must be provided, 
along with estimated cost savings. The proposal should also include implementation 
feasibility, required modifications, investment estimate, and expected return on investment 
(ROI).
